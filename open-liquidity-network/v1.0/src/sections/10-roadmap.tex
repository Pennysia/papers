\section{Roadmap}\label{sec:roadmap}

Table~\ref{tab:roadmap} orders development by technical dependencies and defines a completion criterion for each phase.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering\small
\caption{Proposed rollout and completion criteria.}
\label{tab:roadmap}
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.17\textwidth}p{0.44\textwidth}p{0.31\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Phase} & \textbf{Ships} & \textbf{Exit when} \\
\midrule
\textbf{0. Prototype} \emph{(Ethereum mainnet)} &
Parallel quoting and Settlement with atomic fallback; public contracts and verification reports &
Quote selection and protected execution run on Ethereum mainnet \\
\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{1. Account + multi-chain spot} &
Embedded EVM and Solana wallets, external-wallet support, and same-chain quoting across major EVM networks and Solana &
A user signs in and swaps USDC without a seed phrase or native gas token \\
\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{2. Chainless execution} &
Cross-chain intent execution, unified balances, and broader Settlement deployment &
A cross-chain swap completes within the signed terms through the same account flow \\
\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{3. Regulated lane} &
Verification, regulated venue and fiat-ramp integration, and jurisdiction-aware routing &
An eligible user settles an order through a regulated venue \\
\addlinespace[0.4em]
\textbf{4. Network scale} &
Additional service classes and chain families, with a public order specification and integration APIs &
Third-party fillers and integrators build on the network \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Evaluation will track onboarding completion through the first settled trade, net fill quality against available alternatives, and venue participation in each lane. Execution reporting will measure median and 95th-percentile time from signed-order receipt to verified fill by rail, with time to finality reported separately. Settlement reporting will include fill-verification and failure rates.
