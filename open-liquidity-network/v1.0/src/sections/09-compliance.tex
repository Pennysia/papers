\section{Compliance Posture}\label{sec:compliance}

Verification is optional for access to the network. It enables quotes from regulated venues where the user meets the provider's requirements. Eligibility is checked per venue and per quote, including jurisdictional restrictions, and is displayed before execution.

\label{sec:credential} The goal is a reusable network-level verification credential that reduces repeated onboarding where venues accept it. Identity documents remain with the verification provider and the venue's own onboarding process; Pennysia coordinates credential status.

\begin{tbd}
Venue acceptance will determine whether verification uses a shared attestation or separate onboarding with synchronized status.
\end{tbd}

Regulated coverage expands as venues are onboarded and may remain limited or unavailable in some jurisdictions. Venue-rail settlement carries the counterparty exposure described in Section~\ref{sec:risk}. Pennysia enforces provider eligibility rules but does not determine users' legal or tax status.
