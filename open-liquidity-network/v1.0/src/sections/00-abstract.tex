Pennysia is an open liquidity network that brings external providers into competition for the user's order across chains and services. By comparing executable quotes after costs, it aims to improve the amount users receive while letting them trade through one account and retain control of their assets. The user signs an order specifying the minimum acceptable output, and the network coordinates execution and verifies settlement. Providers integrate through a common interface to reach eligible orders.

\noindent Embedded non-custodial wallets support familiar login methods, while the network handles gas and chain routing. External ramp providers support card and bank transfers. A \textbf{permissionless lane} serves all users through open venues, both onchain and offchain. Optional verification enables access to a \textbf{regulated lane}, subject to each venue's requirements. Pennysia connects these services without operating an underlying liquidity venue.

\noindent An experimental Ethereum deployment demonstrates parallel quoting across 30 venues and enforcement of signed terms with atomic fallback. Pennysia v1, the Open Liquidity Network, extends this design to multiple chain families and services beyond spot trading.
