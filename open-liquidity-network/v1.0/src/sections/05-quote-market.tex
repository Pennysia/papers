\section{The Quote Market}\label{sec:quote-market}

The quote market sends an order to eligible venues in parallel and compares their executable offers. Selection accounts for costs and the likelihood of settlement, both of which affect the user's realized output~\cite{solverwelfare}.

\subsection{The auction}

For user $u$, each venue $v$ in the eligible set $E(u)$ returns a quote $(y_v, c_v, p_v)$, where $y_v$ is gross output, $c_v$ is the all-in cost, and $p_v$ is fill probability. Quotes must be executable commitments received within the market's response window $\tau$. The simplified selection rule below expresses expected net output; the full ranking also accounts for settlement latency and per-quote eligibility requirements.

\begin{equation}
E(u) \;=\; \mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{perm}} \;\cup\; \mathbf{1}[\mathrm{verified}(u)]\cdot \mathcal{V}_{\mathrm{reg}},
\qquad
v^{*}(o) \;=\; \underset{v \in E(u)}{\arg\max}\; \underbrace{p_v \cdot (y_v - c_v)}_{\mathrm{net\; output}}
\end{equation}

\noindent The OLN extends the prototype's gas-aware quote comparison across chains and venue classes.

\subsection{Two axes of liquidity}\label{sec:lanes}

Providers differ by \textbf{permission}, which determines who may use a service, and \textbf{location}, which describes where its liquidity is held. These properties are independent; a venue can require verification while settling through an onchain contract.

Permission determines the user's \emph{lane}. The \textbf{permissionless lane} includes open venues and the full prototype venue set. The \textbf{regulated lane} becomes available through optional verification, subject to venue eligibility (Section~\ref{sec:compliance}). Both participate in the same auction and ranking.

Liquidity location informs the execution rail (Section~\ref{sec:execution}). Atomic and intent rails support onchain settlement, while venue rails coordinate delivery from offchain providers.

\subsection{Chainless quoting}

The user selects the assets and desired outcome. The routing layer resolves the execution path across supported chains and venues, presenting net output and settlement time for review. Chain and rail details remain available for inspection.
