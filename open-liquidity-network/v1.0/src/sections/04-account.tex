\section{The Account}\label{sec:account}

The account is where the CEX/DEX line is deliberately blurred. Signup reads like a centralized exchange. Custody is a self-custody wallet.

\subsection{Onboarding and custody}

A user creates an account with a Web2 login (email, Google, or Apple). No seed phrase, no browser extension. Provisioning creates \textbf{embedded wallets}, one per supported chain family (EVM and Solana at launch). The wallets are real onchain addresses belonging to the user. Assets sent to them are theirs, and every order signed is a standard wallet signature. A Pennysia account is a key management scheme with a login attached, not a database balance. Table~\ref{tab:authority} draws the boundary.

The load-bearing property is that session verification exists to assemble quotes and operate the account, never to move funds. Every value-moving operation traces to a signature produced in the user's session.

\subsection{Gas and wallet choice}

Because execution is signature-first (Section~\ref{sec:execution}), gas is paid by fillers, venues, or relayers and priced into the quote. A user holding only USDC can trade it for output on any chain without acquiring a native token. Where a user's own onchain action is unavoidable (e.g., a first approval), the network sponsors or abstracts the gas rather than assigning a chain-specific errand.

Embedded wallets are the onboarding path, not the only path. An external wallet (extension, WalletConnect, hardware) reaches the identical quote market and execution fabric. The account then acts as a unified view and session.

\subsection{In and out}

On- and off-ramps are a service class, not a feature Pennysia builds. Ramp providers (cards, bank transfers, Apple Pay, Google Pay) return executable quotes for fiat $\leftrightarrow$ token conversion and compete in the same auction (Section~\ref{sec:quote-market}). Fiat rails are regulated by nature, so they sit in the regulated lane. The optional verification credential (Section~\ref{sec:credential}) unlocks them. Cash in, cash out. Never a custodial balance in between.

Recovery is the wallet provider's domain. Re-authentication restores key shares without Pennysia involvement. Embedded wallets are ordinary onchain addresses and orders are standard signed payloads, so a user who leaves keeps full control. No exit cost by design.
